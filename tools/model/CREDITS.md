# 3D body model: assets and licences

The two body models (`public/models/body-male.glb`, `public/models/body-female.glb`) are generated
by the scripts in this folder from the sources below. Nothing is bought; everything is open.

| What | Source | Licence | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| MPFB 2 (MakeHuman Plugin for Blender) 2.0.x | https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/mpfb/ | GPL-3.0 (the tool) | Generating the bodies and the rig. Meshes made with MPFB/MakeHuman are CC0. |
| MakeHuman base mesh, targets, "default" rig and weights | shipped with MPFB | CC0 1.0 | Body shape, skeleton (163 bones) and skin weights |
| makehuman_system_assets pack (skins `young_caucasian_male/female`, eyes `low-poly` + `brown`, eyebrows `eyebrow001`/`eyebrow010`, eyelashes `eyelashes02`, hair `short02`/`ponytail01`) | https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html | CC0 1.0 | Skin texture, eyes, brows, lashes, hair |

| Athletic shorts | generated by `build_bodies.py` from the CC0 base mesh skin | CC0 1.0 | Shorts on both bodies |

## Licence of the generated models

The body meshes, rig, weights, textures and clothing are CC0. The **muscle map** baked into the
models is derived from Z-Anatomy, so `body-male.glb` and `body-female.glb` as distributed are
**CC BY-SA 4.0**, with attribution to Z-Anatomy and BodyParts3D. The app's Credits screen
carries that attribution. If you ever need CC0-only models, build them without Stage B
(skip `bake_muscles.py`); highlighting then has no muscle data.
